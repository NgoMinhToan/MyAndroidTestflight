# Bundled fonts

The Android app bundles the following fonts. Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License
1.1; the full license texts ship inside the APK at `res/raw/ofl_plus_jakarta_sans.txt` and
`res/raw/ofl_ibm_plex_mono.txt`.

| Family | Files | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Plus Jakarta Sans | `plus_jakarta_sans_{regular,medium,semibold,bold,extrabold}.ttf` | https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans (`fonts/ttf`, commit at download time) |
| IBM Plex Mono | `ibm_plex_mono_{regular,medium}.ttf` | https://github.com/IBM/plex (`packages/plex-mono/fonts/complete/ttf`) |

Plus Jakarta Sans: Copyright 2020 The Plus Jakarta Sans Project Authors.
